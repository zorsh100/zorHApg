Privacy policy

House Nest Collector is a personal, single-user project. It is used only by its author, on their own Nest thermostat, and is not distributed or offered to any other person.

Data accessed: ambient temperature, humidity, HVAC mode/status, and setpoints from a Nest thermostat via Google's Smart Device Management API.

Use of data: this data is stored locally, as append-only log files, on hardware owned by the author, solely to build a private thermal model of one house. It is never sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.
